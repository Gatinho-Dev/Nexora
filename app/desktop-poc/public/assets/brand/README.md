# Nexora brand assets

The logo is built around a continuous relay shaped as an `N`: a direct expression of real-time messages, voice, video, and community channels meeting in one place.

## Files

- `nexora-mark.svg`: transparent standalone mark for light surfaces.
- `nexora-app-icon.svg`: dark rounded-square icon for an app, favicon, or notification surface.
- `nexora-logo-light.svg`: full lockup for light backgrounds.
- `nexora-logo-dark.svg`: full lockup for dark backgrounds.

## Brand colors

- Nexora cobalt: `#4654D8`
- Nexora cobalt light: `#7383FF`
- Nexora night: `#11131A`
- Nexora cloud: `#F4F7FB`

Keep the mark flat. Do not add gradients, shadows, glow, outlines, or a second accent color. Leave clear space of at least one vertical stem-width around it.
